# BD-Net Scientometric Analysis

This repository contains all the analyses produced for the scientific paper titled "A collaborative approach to bipolar disorders
science: the relevance of a research network". They consists in a scientometric analysis of scientific output and collaboration patterns
before and after the establishment of the **BD-Net** collaborative
research network (2009), focused on bipolar disorder research in the
EU. The analysis covers:

- Publication trends over time (pre vs. post BD-Net, all authors vs.
  EU-affiliated authors vs. BD-Net authors)
- Interrupted time-series / segmented regression and effect-size
  statistics on the 2009 network inception
- Co-authorship network structure (density, clustering, connected
  components) among BD-Net authors, before vs. after 2009
- Author productivity, normalized by career length
- Country-level collaboration networks within the EU
